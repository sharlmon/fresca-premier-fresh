PremierFood Safety
04 / 07
Every worker, every day

Clean hands.
Safe food.

Wash your hands

Before you start, after every break, and after using the toilet.

Hair net and clean coat

Hair fully covered. Clean coat on and buttoned. Closed shoes.

No jewellery, watches or phones

Leave them in your locker. Nothing loose near the produce.

No eating, drinking or smoking

Only in the rest area, never at the packing tables.

Cut or unwell? Tell your supervisor

Cover cuts with a blue plaster. Report any illness before you start work.

Keep your table clean

Clear waste, wipe down, and keep crates off the floor.

PremierQuality & Packing
05 / 07
Our standard

Grade it.
Pack it.
Trace it.

Every pack carries our promise. Quality is everybody's job.

Check every pod

Fresh, firm, bright green. Remove anything damaged, soft, or off-colour.

Sort by grade

Beans: extra fine and fine. Keep each grade separate.

Pack to specification

Right weight, right pack, right count: 12 × 250 g where the order says so.

Label and record

Correct label on every carton. Traceability starts with you.

Keep it cool

Never leave produce in the sun. Move packed boxes to the cold room quickly.

Not sure? Ask your supervisor before you pack.

PremierWorkplace Safety
06 / 07
Look after yourself and each other

Safety first,
always.

Lift with your legs

Bend your knees, keep your back straight. Ask for help with heavy crates.

Stack crates safely

Stack straight and stable, never higher than the marked line.

Keep walkways clear

No crates, boxes or bags in the aisles. Walk, never run.

Wet floor? Clean it up

Wipe up spills at once and tell your supervisor.

Know your exits

Find the nearest fire exit and assembly point. Never block a door.

Report every accident

Tell your supervisor about injuries and near misses straight away.
