PremierCrates & Handling
Look after every crate

Crates:
handle with care.

Stack straight

Same-size crates together, never higher than the marked line.

Fill, do not overfill

Keep the top clear so the produce is never crushed.

Clean crates only

No cracks, no dirt, no old produce. Damaged crates go to your supervisor.

Lift together

Full crates are heavy. Bend your knees and ask for help.

Crates off the floor

Use pallets or racks, never the bare floor.
