PremierFood Safety
Every worker, every day

Clean hands.
Safe food.

Wash your hands

Before you start, after every break, and after using the toilet.

Hair net and clean coat

Hair fully covered. Clean coat on and buttoned. Closed shoes.

No jewellery, watches or phones

Leave them in your locker. Nothing loose near the produce.

No eating, drinking or smoking

Only in the rest area, never at the packing tables.

Cut or unwell? Tell your supervisor

Cover cuts with a blue plaster. Report any illness before you start work.

Keep your table clean

Clear waste, wipe down, and keep crates off the floor.
