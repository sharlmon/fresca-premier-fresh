Our standard

Grade it.
Pack it.
Trace it.

Every pack carries our promise. Quality is everybody's job.

Check every pod

Fresh, firm, bright green. Remove anything damaged, soft, or off-colour.

Sort by grade

Beans: extra fine and fine. Keep each grade separate.

Pack to specification

Right weight, right pack, right count: 12 × 250 g where the order says so.

Label and record

Correct label on every carton. Traceability starts with you.

Keep it cool

Never leave produce in the sun. Move packed boxes to the cold room quickly.

Not sure? Ask your supervisor before you pack.
